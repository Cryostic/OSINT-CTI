## Why This Message Is Provably Not an Official MMDA Notification

MMDA has published explicit invariants for legitimate NCAP notifications ([PIA, 2026-02-04](09.%20References.md); [PIA, 2026-08-04](09.%20References.md); [Inquirer, 2026-07-21](09.%20References.md)):

| Official invariant (Fact, from MMDA advisories) | This message | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| SMS sender ID is **only** `MMDA_NCAP` (email is only `no-reply@mmda.gov.ph`) | Sender is `el-mashharawi@live[.]de` | ❌ Fails |
| **No payment links** in notifications | Contains `ncapmmda[.]skin/` | ❌ Fails |
| **No reply option** ("NO reply to sender") | Instructs recipient to reply "Y" | ❌ Fails |
| **No SMS contact information** | N/A (also fails by design) | ❌ |
| **No registered vehicle owner name** — actually genuine notices DO identify the vehicle via plate number context; a genuine NOV references the vehicle record (plate/conduction no., MV file no.) | No plate number, no MV file, no violation date/time/place, no reference number | ❌ Fails |
| Fines are **fixed**; no interest or late-payment penalties (MMDA Chair Artes, per [Inquirer 2026-07-21](09.%20References.md)) | Threatens "mandatory additional charges" on overdue accounts | ❌ Fails |
| Legitimate verification portal: `mayhulika.mmda.gov.ph` (and eGovPH app) | Directs to `ncapmmda[.]skin` | ❌ Fails |

**Conclusion — Fact (High confidence):** The message is not a genuine MMDA NCAP notification. Every distinguishing characteristic MMDA has published for identifying fake NCAP texts is present in this lure.

## Sender Identifier Analysis

- `el-mashharawi@live[.]de` — a `live.de` (Microsoft consumer email, Germany) address presented as the **SMS sender**, not an email `From:` header. SMS does not originate from arbitrary email addresses; this identifier indicates one of two mechanisms (Assessment, Medium-High confidence):
  1. **Alphanumeric sender-ID spoofing** through a bulk SMS aggregator / compromised SMS gateway or grey route, where the operator set an arbitrary alpha sender string; or
  2. **Email-to-SMS gateway abuse**, where an operator (possibly with a compromised burner/proton-style mailbox, here a Microsoft consumer account) routes an email to a carrier gateway, causing the email address to appear as the SMS origin.
- Either way the identifier is **operator-controlled noise**, not a verified identity. Disposable and trivially changed (see `04. Attribution and Pattern Mapping.md` for the attribution caution).
- **Intelligence value:** Low for attribution; moderate for correlation if the same identifier appears in other reported lures (unknown — no public reporting tying this identifier to other campaigns found; gap G-5).
