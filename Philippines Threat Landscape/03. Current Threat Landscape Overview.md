# Current Landscape Overview

### Threat Environment Characterization

The Philippine Cyber Threat Environment through mid-2026 is characterized by the simultaneous operation of **two distinct threat ecosystems** - each operating with different objectives, toolsets, and operational tempos but increasingly overlapping in target selection:

- **Tier 1 - Nation-State APT Threats:** Long-duration campaigns targeting Government Sovereignty, Intelligence, Critical Infrastructure, and Military-adjacent Sectors. Primary actors: Earth Preta (PRC), Lazarus Group (DPRK). _**Motivation:**_ Strategic Intelligence Collection, Geopolitical Positioning, and in the DPRK case, Financial Acquisition.

- **Tier 2 - Organized Cybercrime:** High-tempo Ransomware and Financial Fraud Operations targeting Private Enterprise, Healthcare, Financial Services, and Hospitality Sectors. Primary actors: LockBit 5.0, Qilin, Tengu, Thegentlemen. _**Motivation:**_ Financial eExtortion.

### Threat Tempo Assessment
Intelligence indicates a notable **acceleration in ransomware incident tempo** from Q4 2025 through Q2 2026, with confirmed incidents shifting from quarterly to near-monthly frequency. Nation-state espionage activity has maintained a consistent operational tempo aligned with geopolitical developments in the South China Sea and Regional Diplomatic Cycles.
