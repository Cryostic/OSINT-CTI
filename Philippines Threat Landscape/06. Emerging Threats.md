# Emerging Threat 

### I. AI-Augmented Social Engineering

Intelligence from the collection period indicates an escalating trend in AI-enabled social engineering targeting Philippine Financial Institutions and Enterprise Organizations. Techniques identified include Voice Cloning (Vishing), AI-generated phishing content, and "True Login Phishing" methods that abuse insecure API endpoints to automate authentication code theft.

**Assessment:** This threat is assessed as _**Emerging - Likely to Escalate**_ over the 90-day Forecast Horizon, consistent with Regional and Global trends documented in the INTERPOL APAC Cyber Threat Assessment (2025–2026).


### II. Transnational Online Scam Compounds

Intelligence confirms the Philippines' integration into the broader Southeast Asian Ecosystem of Industrialized Transnational Online Scam Compounds. These operations, generating estimated billions annually across the region, introduce complex jurisdictional and law enforcement interdependencies that limit Philippine agencies' ability to independently disrupt associated cyber infrastructure.


### III. Supply Chain and SaaS Dependency Exploitation

Earth Preta's confirmed use of Adversary-in-the-Middle SSO kits to harvest credentials from Cloud SaaS Platforms signals an evolution in the group's targeting toward supply chain and identity provider exploitation. Organizations relying on federated identity and Cloud SaaS Ecosystems face elevated risk of credential compromise propagating across multiple downstream systems.
