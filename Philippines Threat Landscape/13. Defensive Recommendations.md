# Defensive Recommendations

### I. Immediate Actions

- **Priority 1 - Emergency Patch Verification:** Conduct an enterprise-wide audit to confirm all React 19.x deployments have been patched against CVE-2025-55182. Any unverified instance should be treated as compromised pending forensic review. Retroactively audit server logs from December 2025 for React2Shell IOC patterns.

- **Priority 2 - Gogs Platform Remediation:** Verify all Gogs instances are running the post-CVE-2025-8110 symbolic link containment patch. Restrict access to Gogs repositories to authorized users only. Consider migrating to maintained alternatives if organizational patching cadence cannot be sustained.

- **Priority 3 - Phishing Resilience Uplift:** Deploy advanced email filtering with URL sandboxing for Google Drive and cloud storage links. Brief staff on Earth Preta's spear-phishing methodology, particularly the use of seemingly legitimate cloud-hosted delivery URLs. Implement DMARC, DKIM, and SPF enforcement on all organizational domains.

---

### II. Near-Term Actions
**Identity and Access Management Hardening (D3FEND: Credential Hardening):**

- Audit and harden Single Sign-On configurations to detect and block Adversary-in-the-Middle Session Hijacking.
- Implement Phishing-Resistant MFA (FIDO2/hardware tokens) for all privileged accounts and internet-facing services.
- Monitor for token anomalies and implement session binding controls.

**DLL Side-Loading Detection (D3FEND: Process Spawn Analysis):**

- Deploy application allowlisting to prevent unauthorized DLL loading from non-standard paths.
- Audit and baseline legitimate DLL loading behavior for commonly abused legitimate executables.

**Ransomware Resilience (D3FEND: Backup / Restore):**

- Implement the 3-2-1 backup strategy with verified offline/air-gapped copies.
- Conduct tabletop ransomware exercises for critical sector organizations (Healthcare, Financial, Energy).
- Review and segment network to limit lateral movement capability following initial access.

---

### III. Strategic Actions
**Threat Intelligence Integration:**

- Establish a formal CTI Capability or engage a Managed Threat Intelligence provider to maintain situational awareness of Earth Preta, Lazarus Group, LockBit 5.0, and Qilin operational activity.
- Subscribe to INTERPOL-Affiliated Threat sharing mechanisms for Southeast Asia-Specific Intelligence.

**Zero Trust Architecture Adoption:**

- Initiate Zero Trust network architecture planning, prioritizing identity verification and microsegmentation for government and financial sector organizations.

**National Coordination:**

- Engage with DICT (Department of Information and Communications Technology) and DICT-CERT on information sharing for the DPWH/APT73 incident and government-wide threat awareness.
- Participate in Regional Cyber Threat Intelligence sharing arrangements (ASEAN CERT Collaboration).
