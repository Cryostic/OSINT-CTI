# Industry Impact Analysis

### I. Government and Public Infrastructure
**Risk Level: CRITICAL**

- Nation-State Actors (Earth Preta, APT73) actively target government networks for intelligence collection and data compromise.
- The confirmed DPWH Intrusion (March 2026) demonstrates government agencies' vulnerability to lateral exploitation through web-facing infrastructure.
- Geopolitical context elevates the strategic sensitivity of any government data compromise.
- Secondary risk: Compromised Government credentials enabling downstream SaaS and partner network exploitation.

**Threat Actors:** Earth Preta, APT73

---

### II. Telecommunications and Business Process Outsourcing (BPO)
**Risk Level: HIGH**

- Earth Preta's adversary-in-the-middle SSO campaigns directly target the credential infrastructure underpinning telecommunications and BPO sector operations.
- High density of privileged access to enterprise SaaS environments creates high-value credential targets.
- Successful credential compromise in this sector propagates risk to downstream corporate clients of BPO organizations.

**Threat Actors:** Earth Preta 

---

### III. Hospitality and Private Commerce 
**Risk Level: HIGH**

- LockBit 5.0 has directly targeted Philippine Hospitality Entities, with the Feliz Hotel Boracay attack (June 2026) representing the most recent confirmed incident.
- Multi-extortion model creates compounded financial, reputational, and regulatory impact.
- SMEs in Hospitality and Commerce typically lack mature incident response capabilities, increasing the probability of ransom payment.

**Threat Actors:** LockBit 5.0, Qilin

---

### IV. Financial and Banking Services 
**Risk Level: HIGH**

- Philippine Savings Bank / Metrobank Group confirmed as a Qilin ransomware victim (January 2026), demonstrating credible threat actor access to Philippine financial sector networks.
- AI-Augmented Social Engineering (Vishing, Voice Cloning, True Login Phishing) represents an emerging and rapidly escalating attack vector against banking customers and staff.
- Regulatory reporting obligations and reputational sensitivity amplify the business impact of any confirmed incident.

**Threat Actors:** Qilin, Lazarus Group (Financial Theft), AI-augmented Criminal Actors

---

### V. Healthcare
**Risk Level: SIGNIFICANT**

- B.Care Medical Center confirmed as a Qilin victim (May 2026), demonstrating healthcare sector exposure.
- Healthcare organizations possess high-value Personal Health Information (PHI) with strong extortion leverage.
- Operational disruption from ransomware in healthcare environments carries direct patient safety implications.

**Threat Actors:** Qilin 

---

### VI. Energy, Mining, and Industrial
**Risk Level: SIGNIFICANT**

- Philex Mining confirmed as a Qilin victim (April 2026).
- PTT Philippines listed on ransomware tracking registries (March 2026), indicating threat actor interest in the energy sector.
- Critical infrastructure status of energy assets elevates National Security implications of successful attacks.

**Threat Actors:** Qilin, Earth Preta
