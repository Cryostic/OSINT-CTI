# Geographic Threat Assessments

### I. Philippines National Threat Environment

The Philippines occupies a structurally elevated threat position within the Southeast Asian Cyber Threat Landscape driven by three converging factors:

- **Factor 1 - Geopolitical Conflict Nexus:** The Philippines' territorial dispute with the PRC in the South China Sea creates a sustained, politically-motivated driver of Nation-State Cyber activity that has no near-term resolution trajectory. This factor is assessed as _**Persistent and Escalating**_ over the 180-day forecast horizon.

- **Factor 2 - Economic Development and Digital Infrastructure Growth:** The Philippines' expanding Digital Economy, BPO Industry, and Financial Technology Sector create attractive, high-value targets for both Criminal and State-Sponsored Actors. Rapid digitization frequently outpaces security capability maturation.

- **Factor 3 - Regional Cybercrime Ecosystem Integration:** INTERPOL's Operation SECURE and regional intelligence confirm the Philippines' integration into broader Southeast Asian Cybercriminal Infrastructure Networks, exposing the domestic environment to externally-orchestrated campaign spillover.
